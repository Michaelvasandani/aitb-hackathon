# AITB Sponsor ICP by Event Type

AITB (AI Trailblazers) is a Tucson-anchored nonprofit focused on AI workforce development, builder community, and applied AI education. Sponsors are typically motivated by one of four things: recruiting access, brand presence with builders, product distribution to early adopters, or community / ESG credit.

## Revenue Gate (read first)

A sponsor writes a check. A partner gives time, space, mentors, or promotion. These are not the same and must not be mixed in the same list. Before considering any candidate as a sponsor, confirm it can actually pull money out of a budget:

- For-profit company with $5M+ revenue (use Crunchbase, ZoomInfo, public filings, or 50+ employees on LinkedIn as proxy)
- Venture fund or corporate venture arm (any size; they have ecosystem brand budget)
- Foundation with a documented tech / workforce-development grant program (verify on their site or 990, not "they did one grant once")
- Government agency only when a specific named grant program applies (SBIR, NSF EAGER, AZ Commerce workforce grant)

Anything failing this gate, no matter how mission-aligned, is an in-kind partner, not a sponsor. Small nonprofits do not sponsor other nonprofits. Community colleges, chambers of commerce, university departments, and city / county agencies belong in the In-Kind Partners section with a partner ask, not a sponsor ask.

Universities are a special case: the corporate-engagement office, foundation arm, or college dean discretionary fund can sponsor; individual professors and small centers cannot. If targeting a university, name the corporate-engagement office, not the department.

## Hackathon Sponsor ICP

**Best fit:**
- AI tooling / platform companies (model providers, infra, dev tools, vector DBs, agent frameworks). They want builders trying their product. Title sponsor for these is usually $10K to $25K cash plus API credits.
- Cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure, Oracle Cloud). Credits + cash combo. Often routed through their startup programs (AWS Activate, Google for Startups).
- Companies hiring AI engineers in AZ. They sponsor for recruiting access; expect them to want resume access or a recruiter table.
- Local AZ enterprises with internal AI initiatives (Carvana, GoDaddy, Axon, Intel Chandler, Honeywell). Mix of recruiting and community-impact angle.

**Mid fit:**
- AI-adjacent SaaS (legal tech, health tech, edtech) running an AI feature push. They sponsor to be seen as "AI-native" by builders.
- Consulting firms with AI practices (Slalom, West Monroe, Deloitte AI). Recruiting plus thought leadership.
- VCs with AZ portfolio interest. Lower cash, high prestige value; useful for in-kind judging and mentoring.

**Low fit (do not pursue for hackathons unless they ask):**
- Companies whose only AI story is a press release about adopting ChatGPT internally.
- Pure services firms with no developer audience.
- Companies in active layoff cycles or obvious cash conservation.

## Workshop / Bootcamp Series Sponsor ICP

**Best fit:**
- Vendors of the tools being taught (e.g., if the bootcamp teaches LangChain, sponsor LangChain or a LangChain-shop). Cash + credits + curriculum support.
- Local employers who would hire bootcamp graduates. Workforce development angle is the strongest pitch.
- Workforce development foundations (Helios Education Foundation, Arizona Community Foundation tech-focused funds).

**Mid fit:**
- Edtech adjacent vendors (Coursera, Udacity, Pluralsight). Co-marketing angle.

## Summit / Conference Sponsor ICP

**Best fit:**
- Enterprise AI companies wanting executive-level brand presence. Higher price points ($25K+) because the audience is decision-makers, not builders.
- Service firms targeting CIO / CDO audiences.

**Note:** Summit sponsorship economics differ. Use a different sponsor deck and price ladder.

## Meetup Sponsor ICP

**Best fit:**
- Local companies wanting to host (in-kind venue + food). Low cost, high frequency.
- Companies wanting to recruit at a specific topic-focused meetup (e.g., if the meetup is about vector search, pitch vector DB vendors).

**Note:** Meetup sponsorship is usually low-touch and low-dollar. Don't burn warm leads on a meetup that should be saved for a hackathon ask.

## Universal Disqualifiers

- Direct competitors to AITB programming (other AI bootcamps in AZ should not be solicited as sponsors; they are competitors or potential partners, different motion).
- Companies with a documented hard "no" in the last 12 months unless the reason no longer applies.
- Companies that have asked to be removed from AITB outreach.
- Anything Aaron has flagged as off-limits (BB sequencing conflict, personal relationship reason, etc).

## Sponsor Motivation Cheat Sheet

When writing rationale, name the motivation explicitly:

| Motivation | What they want | What AITB delivers |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Recruiting | Builder eyeballs, resume access | Hackathon participant list, recruiter table, "sponsored by" placement |
| Product distribution | Devs trying their tool | API credit tier, demo time, judging slot for technical depth |
| Brand / thought leadership | Logo on builder-community materials | Title sponsor placement, keynote slot, post-event content |
| Community / ESG | AZ workforce-development credit | Local-impact metrics, photo coverage, workforce-development narrative |
| Pipeline-relationship building | Doors into AZ tech ecosystem | Intros to AZ tech leaders, AITB network access |

If you cannot name which of these a sponsor would want, do not include them in the warm tier.
