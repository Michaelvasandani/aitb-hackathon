# Evaluating Grant Opportunities

Score a grant opportunity against AITB's mission, capacity, and strategic goals to decide whether to apply.

---

## Step 1: Gather Grant Details

From the grant announcement, RFP, or funder website, extract:

- **Funder name and program**
- **Focus areas / priorities** (exact language)
- **Eligibility requirements** (org type, geography, revenue, age)
- **Grant amount** (range or fixed)
- **Deadline** and application format (online form, LOI, full proposal, multi-stage)
- **Reporting requirements** (frequency, format)
- **Restrictions** (what they won't fund, excluded activities)

If the user provides a URL, read the page. If they paste text, work from that.

---

## Step 2: Gather AITB Context

Follow the "Before You Start" section in the main SKILL.md. At minimum, you need:

- Current programs and recent events with dates and participant counts
- 501(c)(3) status
- Current partnerships
- Any prior relationship with this funder

---

## Step 3: Score Against 7 Criteria

Use this weighted scoring framework. Each criterion is scored 1-10, then weighted.

| Criterion | Weight | What to assess |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Mission Alignment | 25% | How closely does the funder's stated mission overlap with AITB's work? Look at their language, past grantees, and priority areas. |
| Program Fit | 20% | Can AITB deliver through existing programs? Which ones? How directly do they map to what the funder wants? |
| Financial Fit | 15% | Is the grant amount meaningful for AITB? Too small to justify effort? Too large to be realistic for a young org? |
| Eligibility Match | 15% | Does AITB meet all stated requirements? 501(c)(3), geography, org age, revenue thresholds, etc. |
| Effort vs Return | 10% | How much work is the application? LOI vs full proposal vs online form. Multi-stage vs single submission. Reporting burden. |
| Timeline Feasibility | 10% | Can AITB realistically submit a quality application by the deadline? Factor in Aaron's availability and other priorities. |
| Strategic Value | 5% | Beyond the money: does this funder add credibility, open doors, or create a recurring relationship? |

**Scoring calibration:**
- 9-10: Exceptional fit, almost custom-made for AITB
- 7-8: Strong fit with minor gaps
- 5-6: Decent fit, worth considering if capacity allows
- 3-4: Weak fit, significant misalignment
- 1-2: Poor fit, fundamental mismatch

---

## Step 4: Make a Recommendation

Calculate the weighted total (max 100) and recommend:

| Score | Recommendation | Meaning |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| 60+ | **APPLY** | Worth the effort. Proceed to drafting. |
| 40-59 | **BOOKMARK** | Not right now, but worth tracking. Note the next deadline cycle if applicable. |
| Below 40 | **SKIP** | Poor fit. Don't spend time on it. |

---

## Step 5: Write the Evaluation

Use this structure:

```
# Grant Evaluation: [Funder] -- [Program Name]

## Recommendation: [APPLY / BOOKMARK / SKIP]
**Score: [X]/100**

## Summary
[2-3 sentences: why this is or isn't a good fit. Lead with the strongest alignment or the biggest disqualifier.]

## Score Breakdown
| Criterion | Score | Weight | Weighted | Notes |
|-----------|-------|--------|----------|-------|
| Mission Alignment | X/10 | 25% | X.X | [Specific evidence] |
| Program Fit | X/10 | 20% | X.X | [Which programs, how they map] |
| Financial Fit | X/10 | 15% | X.X | [Amount context] |
| Eligibility Match | X/10 | 15% | X.X | [Requirements met/unmet] |
| Effort vs Return | X/10 | 10% | X.X | [Application format, burden] |
| Timeline Feasibility | X/10 | 10% | X.X | [Days until deadline, feasibility] |
| Strategic Value | X/10 | 5% | X.X | [Credibility, relationship potential] |
| **Total** | | | **X.X** | |

## Red Flags
[Anything that could disqualify or significantly complicate the application. Be direct.]

## If Applying
[Only include this section for APPLY or strong BOOKMARK recommendations]
- **Suggested assignee:** Pablo (draft), Aaron (review/submit)
- **Estimated effort:** S/M/L
- **Programs to highlight:** [ranked list with brief rationale]
- **Prior work to reference:** [specific events with dates]
- **Key deadline:** [date and any fallback cycles]
- **Framing suggestion:** [1-2 sentences on how to position the ask]
```

The Notes column in the score table is where the real value lives. Be specific: name programs, cite events, reference funder language. Vague notes like "good alignment" are useless.

---

## Edge Cases

- **Funder has multiple programs:** Evaluate the best-fit program, not the funder as a whole.
- **Multi-stage application:** Score based on the first stage (LOI). Note the full pipeline in Effort vs Return.
- **Deadline already passed:** Check for the next cycle. If recurring, BOOKMARK with the next date.
- **Eligibility uncertain:** Score Eligibility Match lower and flag it in Red Flags. Don't guess.
