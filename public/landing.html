What we hold fixed

Our core values

Everything about how a chapter runs an event is flexible. These are not. They are what makes an AI Trailblazers weekend an AI Trailblazers weekend.

Inclusivity, without an asterisk

You do not need technical skills to take part. When people could not afford their own cloud accounts, we gave them accounts. The door is the whole point.

The full spectrum, in one room

Sessions run from “how to install Claude Code” to advanced topics. Beginners and builders learn in the same building, on the same weekend.

Heart, transparency, empathy

Community feeling is the engine. Run one of these without it and the weekend does not work, however good the logistics are.

We don't leave anyone behind

We share what we know and we hold hands. Human values are what make the technology worth anything at all.

A pipeline, not a party

Every event exists to find new apprentices, new mentors to guide them, and new employers to hire them. That is the reason we do this.

Community over product

The biggest output of a weekend is connection. The things people build matter — the people they meet matter more.
